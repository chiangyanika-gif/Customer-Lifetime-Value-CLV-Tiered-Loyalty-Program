## 📈 Key Insights & Summary
- **Data source:** Online Retail dataset (UCI Machine Learning Repository), Dec 2010 – Dec 2011
- **CLV definition:** Historical gross revenue per customer (Quantity × UnitPrice) from Dec 2010 to 9 Dec 2011. Cancellation invoices and rows with non-positive quantity or price are excluded; purchases cancelled later are not deducted.
- **Tier rule:** Platinum = top 10% of CLV (90th percentile and above), Gold = 70th–90th percentile, Silver = below the 70th percentile
- **Total customers analyzed:** 4,338
- **Total portfolio CLV:** 8,911,407.90

**Customer tiers:**
- 👑 Platinum: 434 customers | 5,469,382.46 total CLV (~61% of total)
- ⭐ Gold: 869 customers | 1,909,866.12 total CLV (~21%)
- 🥈 Silver: 3,035 customers | 1,532,159.32 total CLV (~17%)

**Limitation:** CLV here is a historical value, not a forecast. It is gross revenue, so customers with large orders that were cancelled later may be overstated.
